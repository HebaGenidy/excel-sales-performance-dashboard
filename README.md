📊 Sales Performance Executive Dashboard (Excel)📌 Project OverviewThis project features an interactive Sales Performance Executive Dashboard built using Microsoft Excel. The objective of this dashboard is to analyze revenue distribution, regional performance, sales rep efficiency, and product categories to provide actionable insights for executive decision-making.   🛠️ Tools & Functions UsedData Transformation & Logic: XLOOKUP, COUNTIFS, SUMIFS.Dynamic Interactivity: Slicers (Order Status: Cancelled, Delivered, Shipped | Regions: East, North, South, West).   Data Visualization: Pie Chart, Horizontal Bar Chart, Column Charts with Dual Metrics.   Design & Layout: Custom UI layout with rounded cards and a dark purple color palette.   🎯 Key Business Questions AddressedWhat is the revenue distribution across different geographical regions?   Who are the top-performing sales representatives driving revenue?   How do monthly revenue and total order counts trend over time (Q1 & Q2)?   Which product categories generate the highest revenue?   How can sales performance be filtered dynamically by order status and region?   💡 Key Insights & Analytics Breakdown1. 🌍 Sales Regions Revenue DistributionSouth Region leads the business, generating 29% of the total revenue.
